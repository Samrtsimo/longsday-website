公司简介 Company Profile

宁波隆事达供应链有限公司

Ningbo Longsday Supply Chain

Longsday 是一家立足宁波、辐射全球的国际货运代理公司。我们提供海运、空运、铁路、DDP 税后到门、Amazon FBA 头程及多式联运等一站式物流服务，帮助客户将货物安全、准时、经济地送达世界各地。

Longsday is a Ningbo-based international freight forwarding company with global reach. We provide ocean, air, rail, DDP, Amazon FBA first-mile, and multimodal logistics services — delivering your goods safely, on time, and cost-effectively worldwide.

公司坐落于世界第一大港——宁波舟山港，依托得天独厚的港口资源和十年以上的行业经验，我们已为超过1000家客户提供了可靠、高效的物流服务。我们的团队精通中英双语，熟悉各国海关政策和贸易合规，致力于成为您最值得信赖的物流伙伴。

Located at the world's busiest port — Ningbo-Zhoushan Port — and backed by 10+ years of industry experience, we've served 1000+ clients with reliable, efficient logistics. Our bilingual team is well-versed in global customs regulations and trade compliance, committed to being your most trusted logistics partner.
